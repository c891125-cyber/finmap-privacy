Privacy Policy

中文 · English

📋 Overview

FinMap is a personal expense-tracking app, and we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how your data is handled.

✅ We do not collect personally identifiable information, do not show ads, and do not sell or share your data with third parties.

📦 What data is stored?

FinMap only stores the entries you type in yourself, including:

  • Amount, category and subcategory
  • Notes
  • Date

This data is stored only on your device and in your personal iCloud account.

☁️ iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud, your entries are synced between your devices through Apple CloudKit.

  • The data is stored in your personal iCloud account, and only you can access it
  • We (the developer) cannot access your iCloud data
  • Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud services

💳 Payments

FinMap's one-time purchase is processed by the Apple App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information or credit card details. All transactions are handled by Apple.

🚫 What we don't do

  • We don't collect your name, email address or any personally identifiable information
  • We don't track your usage or location
  • We don't show ads
  • We don't sell or share data with third parties
  • We don't use any analytics or advertising SDKs

🗑️ Deleting your data

You can delete any entry in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all local data from your device. To delete data stored in iCloud, go to Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage on your iPhone.

👶 Children's privacy

FinMap is not designed specifically for children under 13, and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

📬 Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the developer contact page on the App Store.
